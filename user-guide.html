Car Wash & Detailing Management System

Welcome to the complete interactive operations guide for your car wash and automotive detailing business. Use this manual to train staff, understand every sidebar screen, run daily shifts, prevent cash leakage, and troubleshoot issues.

1. System Architecture & Building Blocks

The Car Wash & Care platform operates on an Autonomous Local-First Architecture. Your physical workshop runs on an in-store mini-PC server that continues taking orders, printing receipts, and operating bays even if the internet drops completely.

Pillar Device & Location Core Responsibility
Local Shop Server In-store mini-PC (Windows/XAMPP) Stores the local MySQL database, hosts the POS engine, controls receipt printers, and orchestrates local Wi-Fi terminals.
POS & Bay Tablets Cashier counter & Bay wall mounts High-speed touch interfaces for order intake, queue management, mechanic checklists, and parts requests.
Waiting Lounge TV Smart TV in customer lounge (`tv.php`) Displays real-time bay progress, queue order, and ready-for-pickup notices with audio chimes.
Central Cloud Portal Headquarters Cloud Repository Aggregates multi-store revenue, synchronizes master catalogs, and manages customer memberships.

2. The 5 Unbreakable Operational Rules

Every employee must memorize and respect these five core anti-fraud and quality policies:

Rule 1: "No Receipt, No Wash" (គ្មានវិក្កយបត្រ គ្មានការលាង)
Washers and technicians are strictly forbidden from touching any vehicle without an official printed receipt clipped under the wiper blade or displayed on the dashboard.
Rule 2: Blind Cashiering (ការគិតលុយបិទភ្នែក)
Expected cash sales are hidden from cashiers during their shift. Cashiers must physically count every bill and coin before the system calculates variances.
Rule 3: Immutable Warehouse Ledger (បញ្ជីឃ្លាំងមិនអាចកែប្រែបាន)
Stock records cannot be edited or deleted (`SQLSTATE 45000`). Discrepancies are resolved solely by adding Manager-signed Correction Lines.
Rule 4: Manager PIN Overrides (ការអនុញ្ញាតដោយលេខកូដអ្នកគ្រប់គ្រង)
Cashiers cannot apply manual discounts, cancel tickets, or pop open the cash drawer without a Manager PIN.
Rule 5: Soft Deactivation Only (អសកម្មបុគ្គលិក ដោយមិនលុបទិន្នន័យ)
Departed staff accounts are set to `inactive`, never hard-deleted, permanently preserving historical audit trails and commission records.

3. The Complete Customer Journey (Arrival to Exit)

Follow the end-to-end flow of every vehicle that enters your car wash:

1

Vehicle Pulls In & License Plate Lookup Cashier

Cashier presses F2 on the POS. Type the plate (e.g. 2A-1234). If returning, their profile, loyalty tier, and next oil due load instantly. If new, tap [+ New Customer], enter name, phone, and vehicle size.

2

SmartQueue Wait Estimate Check SmartQueue

The system calculates estimated wait time based on vehicle size and bay occupancy. If the line exceeds 45 minutes, an amber banner prompts the cashier to offer an appointment booking or VIP P2 Express wash.

3

Cart Builder & Contextual Upsells Cashier

Add services. Prices automatically adapt to vehicle size (Small Car vs SUV vs Pickup). POS displays upsell pills (e.g. "Offer Ceramic Tire Wax +$2" or "Oil Change Due!").

4

Checkout & Slip Upload Cashier

Customer chooses Cash (USD/KHR), ABA Pay, or KHQR Scan. For digital transfers, cashier enters the reference number and snaps a photo of the bank confirmation slip.

5

Receipt Printing & Queue Placement Printer & TV

Receipt prints with bold Queue # (e.g. #Q-014). Receipt is placed under windshield wiper. Waiting Lounge TV (tv.php) displays car in progress.

6

Bay Operations & The Handshake Washer & Mechanic

Washer taps [Start Job]. Once dry, taps [Finish Car]. On mixed orders (Wash + Oil), the vehicle triggers the Handshake Protocol: Mechanic clicks [Receive From Wash] and drives car to Bay 4 for servicing.

7

Completion Chime & Key Handover TV & Cashier

TV board chimes and moves vehicle to "Ready for Pick-up". Cashier returns keys to customer with a smile.

5. Money Controls, Shifts, and Cashier Security

A. Shift Opening & The Blind Cashiering Principle

Every shift begins with counting physical bills in the drawer to establish the Opening Float (e.g. $50 + 200,000៛). During the entire shift, the system hides expected sales from the cashier. This forces staff to perform an honest physical count at shift end.

B. The Two-Cashier Shift Handover Formula

At shift end, the outgoing cashier enters their counted cash and confirms with their PIN. The incoming cashier signs off with their PIN. The system runs:

Expected Cash = Opening Float + Cash Sales - Drawer Expenses - Cash Refunds
Variance = Counted Cash - Expected Cash

If Variance < 0, the shift is flagged as a SHORTAGE. The deficit is recorded as owed by the outgoing cashier and logged in `audit_logs`.

C. Day Closing & The 23:59:00 Cutoff

At the end of the operating day, the Manager opens Close Day (Z-Report), counts final safe cash, and generates the official Z-Report. At 23:59:00, the system automatically locks registers to prevent unauthorized after-hours sales.

6. Error Codes & Troubleshooting Table

Quick lookup guide for every error message you may encounter on tablets or screens:

Error Code What It Means Step-by-Step Fix
DEVICE_BLOCKED Tablet was locked in Back Office. Owner opens POS Devices screen and clicks [Unblock Device].
DEVICE_KEY_REQUIRED Tablet cache was cleared; pairing key lost. Open pos/index.html#pair and re-enter the pairing key from POS Devices.
ACCOUNT_LOCKED 5 wrong PIN attempts entered. Wait 10 minutes for security cooldown, or have Manager reset attempts in Staff Management.
DAY_LOCKED Time is ≥ 23:59:00 or day was closed. Manager enters Manager PIN in Shift Management to close day or unlock with mandatory reason.
DISCOUNT_EXCEEDS_TOTAL Discount entered is higher than cart total. Correct the discount figure so it does not exceed the ticket subtotal.
Printer Port 1818 Error Thermal receipt printer offline or cable loose. Check printer power/paper, ensure USB is connected, or use the browser print fallback (Ctrl+P).
Cash Drawer Won't Pop Drawer cable unplugged or locked. Ensure RJ11 cable is plugged into the printer's DK port; turn physical key to vertical position.
TV Shows "Code Incorrect" TV URL parameter does not match secret code. Check Settings → General for the TV secret code and update the URL: tv.php?code=...

7. Printable Role Quick Reference Cards

Print these 1-page summaries to laminate and attach to workstations:

Cashier (អ្នកគិតលុយ)

Counter
  • Count opening float before tapping [Start Shift]
  • Always search plate first (F2)
  • Select vehicle size type accurately
  • Offer suggested upsells (wax, engine flush)
  • Upload bank slip photo on ABA/KHQR
  • "No Receipt, No Wash" on every vehicle
  • NEVER wash without printed receipt
  • NEVER discount without Manager PIN
  • NEVER refund while offline

Washer (បុគ្គលិកលាង)

Bays 1-3
  • Check windshield receipt before spraying
  • Tap [Start Job] the second car enters bay
  • Clean wheels and apply tire shine evenly
  • Wipe windows streak-free inside and out
  • Perform 4-point dry QC checklist
  • Tap [Finish Car] to chime TV board
  • NEVER wash cars without a receipt
  • NEVER use dropped dirty towels on paint
  • NEVER spray high pressure closer than 30cm

Mechanic (ជាងជួសជុល)

Bay 4
  • Tap [Receive From Wash] on mixed orders
  • Submit digital parts requests for oil/filters
  • Perform digital 12-point inspection
  • Record exact liters used in adjust_oil
  • Record odometer and write next oil sticker
  • Tap [Finish Task] to complete job
  • NEVER take parts without digital request
  • NEVER start engine without checking dipstick
  • NEVER guess oil viscosity specifications

Manager (អ្នកគ្រប់គ្រង)

Floor & Desk
  • Verify all bays online by 07:30 AM
  • Conduct unannounced surprise spot audits
  • Authorize discounts/voids with Manager PIN
  • Investigate cash shortages on shift handovers
  • Generate final Z-Report to close day
  • Scan audit logs daily for unauthorized voids
  • NEVER share Manager PIN with staff
  • NEVER approve offline refunds
  • NEVER ignore cash handover shortages
